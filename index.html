Every window, one hover away.

Point at any app in the Dock to see all of its open windows, like the taskbar on Windows. DockDoor also adds Alt+Tab window switching and keyboard controls, in Liquid Glass. Free, open source, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Free for macOS 13 Ventura and later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

Close, minimize and quit from the preview.

Click a thumbnail to bring that window forward. The buttons on each preview, or a middle-click, act on that window without leaving the app you're in.

  • Every Space, every display

    Windows on other desktops and displays show up too, including minimized and hidden ones.

  • Grid, list or large

    Show windows as thumbnails, a compact list of titles, or large previews. The list can turn on by itself when an app has lots of windows.

  • Close or minimize all

    Buttons on each preview close or minimize every window of that app at once.

Alt+Tab for every window.

Hold ⌥ and press Tab to see every open window, not just every app. Press Tab again to move through them and release to switch. Type to search, or change the shortcut in Settings.

More

Folder Pop

Hover a folder in the Dock to see what's inside. Sort the list, open a file, or drag it straight into another app, without opening a Finder window.

Settings › Dock Previews

Compact list

Show an app's windows as a list of titles instead of thumbnails. Choose how many windows it takes to switch to the list, or use it all the time.

Settings › Appearance

Large previews

Make thumbnails bigger to tell similar windows apart. A thumbnail can also open a full-size preview when you hover it.

Settings › Appearance

Music controls

Hover an app that's playing audio, like Spotify, Apple Music or a browser tab, to get a player instead of thumbnails. Pin it to your desktop, with synced lyrics for Spotify and Apple Music.

Settings › Widgets

Swipe to minimize

Swipe two fingers toward the Dock on a preview to minimize that window, or away from it to maximize. You can also scroll on a Dock icon to show or hide an app, and shake a preview to minimize everything else.

Settings › Gestures & Keybinds

Quick quit

Hold ⌘ and right-click a Dock icon to quit the app, or add ⌥ to force quit it. You can also click the app you're in to minimize its windows, and lock the Dock to one display.

Settings › Dock Previews

Change how it looks.

Choose Liquid Glass (macOS 26 and later), frosted or clear, and set the preview size, spacing, corners and where the window controls sit.

Nothing leaves your Mac.

DockDoor has no servers to send anything to. There are no analytics, no usage tracking and no crash reporters. Debug logs are written locally and go nowhere unless you export them yourself.

The whole app is open source under GPL-3.0. You can read the code, build it yourself, or help translate it.

Questions

Can't find an answer? Ask on Discord or GitHub.

How do I see all the open windows of an app on my Mac?

macOS doesn't show window previews in the Dock on its own. With DockDoor installed, point at an app's icon in the Dock and all of its open windows appear as small pictures. Click the one you want to bring it to the front.

Is there an Alt+Tab for Mac like on Windows?

Command-Tab on a Mac switches between apps, not windows. DockDoor adds Option-Tab, which shows every open window so you can pick the exact one, like Alt+Tab on Windows. You can change the shortcut in Settings.

Is DockDoor easy to set up?

Yes. Download it, drag it to your Applications folder and open it. macOS asks you to allow Accessibility and Screen Recording, which DockDoor needs to find your windows and show their previews. After that, point at any app in the Dock.

Where should I download DockDoor from?

Only from these places:

  • DockDoor Free: dockdoor.net or github.com/ejbills/DockDoor (Homebrew installs from GitHub too)
  • DockDoor Pro: pro.dockdoor.net

DockDoor needs Accessibility and Screen Recording permission to work. An app with that much access from an untrusted source is a real risk: a modified copy could record your screen, log your keystrokes or send your data somewhere. Re-uploaded and cracked copies of DockDoor with malware in them have already turned up on other sites. There's no legitimate free version of DockDoor Pro.

Is DockDoor really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no trial, no subscription and no paywall. It's funded by donations and by people who buy DockDoor Pro, a separate app.

Is DockDoor Pro part of DockDoor Free?

No, they're separate apps. DockDoor Free adds previews, Alt+Tab, gestures and keyboard controls to the built-in macOS Dock. DockDoor Pro replaces the Dock with its own, which has previews, profiles, widgets, pinned content and much more customization.

If Pro is replacing your Dock you usually don't need Free as well. Pro is a $20 one-time purchase at pro.dockdoor.net.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac on macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Liquid Glass styling is available on macOS 26 and later.

Will it slow my Mac down?

It shouldn't. DockDoor is a native app that runs from the menu bar and only captures window thumbnails when a preview or the switcher needs them.

How do I change the shortcuts?

Open DockDoor's settings and go to Gestures & Keybinds. From there you can:

  1. Change the window switcher's modifier (Control, Option or Command) and trigger key, plus set an alternate shortcut.
  2. Set up to three Command-key actions, like close, minimize or quit, for the switcher.
  3. Pick what trackpad swipes do on previews and in the switcher.
  4. Choose what middle-click does on a preview.
Does it work with every app?

Almost every standard Mac app works. A few apps built with unusual UI frameworks may show fewer windows or skip some actions.

How do I get the old, slimmer previews back?
  1. Open DockDoor's settings and go to Dock Previews.
  2. Turn on Allow dynamic image sizing so previews resize to fit each window.
  3. Turn on Embed controls in preview frames for the classic toolbar style.
  4. Optionally, turn off Dock styling on traffic light buttons for a plainer look.

These only change Dock previews. The window switcher stays the same.

How do I send a debug log?
  1. Open DockDoor's settings and go to Support.
  2. Turn on Debug Logging under Updates.
  3. Do whatever causes the problem.
  4. Click Export Logs and attach the file to your GitHub issue or Discord message.

Logs stay on your Mac. Nothing is sent unless you share the file yourself.
